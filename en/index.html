On screen

The lab

How do you know an emulator is faithful to a machine that was never produced? You write a test program, run it on the real C65, photograph the screen and compare.

  • MEGATEST 65: the chips one by one, registers and behaviour
  • SUPERTEST: timings, cycles, DMA and bitplanes
  • DIGITEST: audio samples on the two 8580 SIDs
  • CARICATEST: loading from disk while a game runs

Every difference becomes a fix, recorded in the devlog.
